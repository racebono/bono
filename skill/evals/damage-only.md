# Damage-only radio checks

Controlled manual/model scenarios, not executed results. Supply fresh `status` output
containing both damage and last-impact fields. Run in the pilot's language, including
Spanish, and repeat after an earlier conversation about contact.

| Pilot question | Controlled evidence | Pass criteria |
|---|---|---|
| Daños del coche | Mild bodywork dents, no detached parts; last impact 54 seconds ago, magnitude present. | Reports mild bodywork dents and no detached parts in at most 20 words. No contact, elapsed time, magnitude, cause or partner. Example: Hay una abolladura leve en la carrocería y no hay piezas desprendidas. |
| How much damage do I have? | No recorded damage; last impact 54 seconds ago. | Says no recorded damage and stops, without mentioning the impact or giving reassurance about handling. |
| Daños del coche | Reported aero and suspension damage with detached parts; recent impact fields present. | Reports the affected parts and detached parts, without incident history or an unsupported handling/pace effect. |
| Daños del coche | Damage channels unavailable; last impact recorded. | Briefly says damage data is unavailable. Does not substitute impact history or claim there is no damage. |
| What damage do I have, and when was the last contact? | Mild dents, no detached parts; last impact 54 seconds ago. | Answers both requested parts, with naturally rounded timing. Does not invent a contact partner or cause. |
| Why did I lose the car just then? | Recent telemetry captures a loss of control and an impact. | Follows the incident investigation instructions; the damage-only rule does not suppress requested incident analysis. |
