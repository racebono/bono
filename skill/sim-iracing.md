## Simulator notes (iRacing)

- **Setup:** `setup_now` names are paths (`Section/Corner/Setting`) that vary by car: use the names as given, never ones from another car. Values are display text with units. Some entries are readouts of the last run rather than settings: don't present them as something set. iRacing gives no setup ranges (`step`/`min_step`/`max_step`/`saved_value` are NULL): never say a setting is at its limit or how far it can go.
- **Tyres and ride heights:** while driving, iRacing reports no live tyre surface temperatures, running pressures, or ride heights: say they aren't known. `carcass_c`, `core_c`, `life_left_pct`, and the snapshot's `TYRES last reading` are the last reading, not live values: state their age as at least `reading_age_s` (counted from first seen, so they can be older), don't treat them as current tyres, and take no wear rate from them (`stint_wear_pct_per_lap` and per-lap life are NULL). `cold_pressure_kpa` is the garage setting, not running pressure. Judge current tyres from lap times and car balance.
- **Positions and traffic:** no car has a world position (`ahead_of_player_m`, `right_of_player_m`, raw `position_x/y/z` are NULL; standings places are present). Snapshot `TRAFFIC` gives only iRacing's count of cars alongside and on-track gaps either way (`gap_car_ahead_s`, `gap_car_behind_s`), nothing about other cars' locations: don't call the track clear beyond that count. Rivals' speed, lap starts, sector times, and pit stops are estimated from lap progress; garaged cars have no telemetry rows.
- **Yellows:** no sector yellows (`race_state.local_yellow` is NULL); `cars_now.under_yellow` is the yellow shown to the car.
- **Laps:** `laps_completed` (and `leader_laps_completed`) is `-1` until the car first crosses the line after getting in (out lap or pre-start): no lap is done yet, then it steps from `-1` to `0`. `lap` is `1` on the out lap and stays `1` for the lap after it.
- **Places and gaps:** in races, `time_behind_next_s` and `gap_place_*` are live between cars racing under a lap apart, and `time_behind_leader_s` is live while racing on track (time to reach the leader's spot, whole laps at class pace); cars finished on the winner's lap have exact line gaps. Otherwise (pit lane, off track, unfinished after winner, a lap down at finish, pre-green) gaps are iRacing's own, updated roughly once a lap at a timing line, so they can jump at transitions (`time_behind_leader_s` is NULL while P1 has passed iRacing's leader since the line). Across classes, gaps don't add up to time behind leader. While racing, `position` and `class_position` follow on-track order so passes count immediately, whereas iRacing's own places update at the line: a car out of the world, re-entered, towed, reversed over the line, or not yet across keeps its iRacing order (behind cars iRacing had ahead and any with more laps done) until finishing a lap, and a finished car takes its own a few seconds past the line. Pre-green, and post-flag when finishers aren't clear, places are iRacing's (or grid). Two cars swapping on track while one keeps iRacing's place or is in pits may have no gap or an older one; a car beside the leader can read `0` behind it; a negative `race_gap_to_player_s` for the car ahead in class can be such a car rather than side by side.
- **Best lap and validity:** the best lap is `cars_now.best_lap_s` (iRacing's own, excluding uncounted laps); a quicker `lap_stats` time may be uncounted. Don't call it invalid (iRacing doesn't say why) and don't call any lap valid: iRacing doesn't report lap validity.
- **Engine map:** `cars_now.engine_map` is the fuel mixture.
- **Damage:** mechanical and bodywork damage are unreported (NULL): never say there is no damage.
- **Pit services and fuel estimate:** `body repair` in `pit_services_selected` is iRacing's fast repair (without it a stop still repairs, only slower). `game_fuel_per_lap_l` is fuel used over the last lap's driven distance (slow or yellow running lowers it; NULL until a lap is driven since pits, tow/reset, refuelling, or getting in).
